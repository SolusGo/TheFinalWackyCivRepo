# Artwork

The supplied Werribee auction panorama provides the 1024 × 768 Dawn of Man scene, the 1280 × 960 static diplomacy scene, and Lynne's circular portrait. Each asset uses a separately framed crop of the panorama. The civilization emblem and flag use an emerald and champagne gold house roof and key. The consultant uses the native Great Merchant model.

Built-in ImageGen supplied three additional illustrations. The following prompt briefs describe the delivered assets.

- **Consultant portrait**. A senior Australian real-estate consultant with property documents against a Werribee streetscape, with a crisp silhouette, warm highlights and emerald and champagne-gold accents.
- **Office portrait**. A modern Australian suburban real-estate office with cream brick, emerald trim, property displays, landscaped native flowers and warm morning light.
- **Selection map**. A stylised schematic of Wyndham with agricultural blocks, street grids, the Werribee River and Port Phillip Bay, using emerald relief and champagne-gold roads.

The `WERRIBEE_Portrait*.dds` files contain the emblem, Lynne, Consultant and Office in that order. The `WERRIBEE_Alpha*.dds` files and `WERRIBEE_Flag32.dds` provide transparent crest masks. `WERRIBEE_LeaderScene.xml` references the static diplomacy texture. The map is illustrative.
