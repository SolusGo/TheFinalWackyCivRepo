# Artwork

The packaged DDS assets are under `../Art`. The six illustrated source compositions were created with the built-in image generation tool. The circular crest, white alpha/flag, interface backdrop, frames and tree connectors were drawn procedurally. Art was cropped and assembled into sized DDS atlases for the game. The original generated PNGs are intermediate files.

| DDS and atlas | Illustration prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Dawn | Original 4:3 dark fantasy painting of the obsidian Spider Queen overlooking a subterranean civilization of suspended cities and silk bridges, tiny spiders and egg sacs, with crimson and gold cavern light. No text or border. |
| Diplomacy and Queen | Close portrait of a nonhuman Spider Queen on a royal-cavern obsidian dais with crimson markings, bronze light, web strands, guardians and ivory eggs. No text or border. |
| Map | Antique overhead ink and watercolor cutaway on parchment of branching caverns, silk bridges, chitin citadels, egg clusters and a crimson spider compass. No lettering. |
| Spider | Four square icon motifs for the Black Widow, ochre-striped Broodling, green venom dripping from the Venom Spider's fangs, and the Armoured Spider's layered blue-black shell. Each design fits a near-black square without text. |
| Brood | Four square icon motifs for a cavern full of Brood Chamber eggs, three amber eggs for SP, a crystalline magenta gene spiral for EP, and a silver web ring for CP. |
| Evolution | Nine motifs for two intelligence stages, a venom gland, chitin plates, a toxin skull, healing armor, a crowned apex spider, a poison bubble, and a crimson royal egg. |

The 1024×768 Dawn of Man and 1280×960 diplomacy art are separate scenes. The atlas entries use the civ-specific `ARACHNID_` namespace and ship portrait sizes from 16 to 256 pixels.
