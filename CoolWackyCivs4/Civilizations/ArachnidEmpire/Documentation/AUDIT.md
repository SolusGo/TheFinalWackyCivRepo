# Community Patch v151 hardening audit

The installed standalone manifest identifies version 151 and release 5.4.6. The inspected source is [Release-5.4.6, dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/tree/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba). The locally installed DLL SHA-256 was `90AC07BE0C3D4773E97FA6B1BB1EF528CA9C8633F0AF357E4A34CF2510FB6F93`. The manifest and hash identify the local inputs. No game executable was launched during validation.

## Confirmed issues and changes

- In `Attack`, quick melee can generate its combat information before defensive fire support launches a second combat at the original attacker's plot. The old single battle slot lost the outer battle. Store up to eight generated transactions, select the newest matching target coordinates at the resolver, and discard unfinished entries at the core update boundary. Animated combat resolves damage inline after the visualization request. Queued defensive support regenerates melee when its mission resumes. [Combat resolver and mission branches](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitCombat.cpp)
- The resolver identifies unit attack sources in RED. For city bombardment the attacker owner and ID can both be -1 even though `ResolveRangedCityVsUnitCombat` passes the attacking city's owner to the damage call. The ranged city signature now admits only its exact RED target and still requires a hostile lethal prekill plus native confirmation. RED also supplies unit IDs for principal participants when a corresponding join was absent. Explicit bystander joins remain required for collateral eligibility. [Combat result and city damage paths](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitCombat.cpp)
- A successful interception of an air strike aborts the strike and zeroes both principals' damage. The combat resolver applies the interception damage under the intended target's owner, even when a third party provided the interceptor. For this exact event shape, the native death callbacks still validate the death, while the live RED interceptor determines who gets credit. An unidentifiable interceptor or combined damage fails closed. A failed interception leaves ordinary retaliation attribution intact. [Air combat generation and resolution](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnitCombat.cpp#L1345) · [Interceptor selection](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlot.cpp#L3718)
- Delayed city-state gifts serialize promotions and the name before removal, then apply them after creating the destination unit. The path can bypass `UnitConverted`. All six private promotions now set CP's `LostOnGifting`, and deferred creation reconciliation removes inherited traits and generated names even on a returned gift. Only our successful spawn and a verified same-owner upgrade authorize traits on a newly created unit. The one-time load reconciliation repairs previously leaked foreign traits and orphaned poison markers. The Arachnid owner still uses native specialization promotions as the reservation. Multiple markers resolve in Broodling, Venom, Armoured declaration order during reconciliation. [Gift application](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvMinorCivAI.cpp)
- `InitUnit` fires creation callbacks and does not enforce purchase eligibility. The spawn transaction now checks that the returned ID belongs to a new live unit of the expected type and owner, on the selected legal plot, with no stacking conflict or post-creation strategic resource deficit. CP accounts for the unit's resource use during initialization. A rejected new unit is removed without a killer before SP and cooldown are committed. An existing ID returned by a faulty creation callback is left alone. [Lua wrapper](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/Lua/CvLuaPlayer.cpp) · [Unit initialization and removal](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvUnit.cpp)

## Contracts retained and tightened

| Hook | 5.4.6 contract and use |
| --- | --- |
| `BattleStarted(kind,x,y)`, `BattleJoined(owner,id,role,isCity)`, `BattleFinished()` | Enabled by `EVENTS_BATTLES`. Principal roles are attacker 0, defender 1, and interceptor 2. Other joins cannot replace a principal. Ordinary melee, ranged, air and sweep finish after their damage resolution. |
| `CombatResult(a,aID,damageToA,finalA,maxA,d,dID,damageToD,finalD,maxD,i,iID,interception,x,y)` | RED pre-damage callback after the veto. The damage-to fields are indexed by recipient. Venom requires positive damage from the correct principal plus surviving target damage growth at finish. |
| `UnitPrekill(owner,id,type,x,y,delayed,killer)` then `UnitKilledInCombat(killer,owner,type,killingUnitID)` | Lethal damage updates HP before `kill(true,killer)`; prekill precedes `startDelayedDeath`. The confirmation follows synchronously with killing unit ID -1 from this path. Match by killer, victim owner and type, then consume the exact participant ID once. More than one participant can be credited in the same transaction. Multiple incompatible pending matches of the same tuple fail closed. |
| `CombatEnded(…)` and `GameCoreUpdateEnd()` | RED end follows the resolver and its completion hook. It drops the resolving transaction without erasing separately generated combat. Core update end drops unfinished combat and processes only queued new units. |
| `UnitCreated(owner,id,type,x,y)`, `UnitConverted(oldOwner,newOwner,oldID,newID,isUpgrade)` | The first queues post-copy sanitation and clears a reused ID's pending poison. The second follows promotion copy and precedes native removal of the old unit. Same-owner upgrades preserve specialization, derived traits and CP. Direct gifts and CP conversions strip traits. Native capture paths that create a fresh unit receive the same sanitation. |
| `PlayerDoTurn(owner)` | In 5.4.6 this runs before `doTurnUnits`. A poison record is consumed on the next callback for its owner regardless of that owner's relative position in the turn order. The marker, creation turn and still-live unit must match. The HP floor and schema 1 layout are unchanged. |
| `CityCaptureComplete(old,wasCapital,x,y,new,pop,conquest,works,capturedWorks)` | Common `acquireCity` notification, including non-conquest transfers. Always sanitize the destination city. Original city identity remains coordinates, original owner and founding turn. Elimination removes units through native prekill. Progress is retained for resurrection and a different liberated city cannot substitute for the original capital. |

The pinned [event declarations](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CustomMods.h) and [player implementation](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/dcb33a654cd9e8efb038a0733b4025e19cbcd8ba/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlayer.cpp) also support the existing city, era and turn integration. Initialization logs a diagnostic and disables the console when its required CP options cannot be confirmed. Existing global options are only enabled, so the mod does not disable other packs' hooks.

## Limits and offline verification

- Native source supports the same paired damage path for ordinary offensive and defensive land/naval combat, city bombardment, aircraft and matched interception. Capture and overrun callbacks with different ordering, paradrop interception, nuclear paths, and splash deaths without an identified participant are outside this attribution. Venom also excludes collateral and a hit that is masked by a same-combat heal with no surviving net damage increase. Third-party code that overwrites unit state without using native creation or conversion hooks remains outside the transfer contract.
- The contemporary class allowlists and obsolescence fallback policy are unchanged. The database validator checks the actual Black Widow and Barracks baselines, ranged progression through Bazooka, and exact defensive and evolutionary promotion values. Resource checks use the native player ledger and the active database's primary/additional technology and quantity requirements.
- Tests cover both the older schema 1 reload and new regression cases for defensive fire support, city slots missing in RED, third-party air interception from both sides, failed and unidentified interceptors, simultaneous transaction participants, no qualifying callback, duplicate notifications, poisoned ID reuse, mismatched markers, gifts, rollback failures, availability races and capital recovery after elimination. The first eligible load performs one roster sanitation pass for existing saves. Normal turns do not scan foreign military rosters.
- Remaining live verification includes camera modes, actual animations and callback timing, full naval and air scenarios, other mods' transfer extensions, sale and city liberation in the UI, pre-audit save loading, and long 34-civilization Marathon games. See [Testing](TESTING.md) for the runtime checklist.
