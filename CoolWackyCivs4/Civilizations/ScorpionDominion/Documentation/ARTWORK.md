# Artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool produced two original source sheets. The scene sheet provides the portrait, Dawn of Man, territory map and Emperor Scorpion portrait. The emblem sheet provides the civilization crest, Molting Chamber, five promotions, War Council and Hall. The unit flag and recolourable civilization alpha atlas use the isolated crest silhouette. The Council panel is a native DDS texture with drawn borders.

The game files are in `Art`. Scene textures use DXT1, and block-aligned alpha atlases use DXT5. The 45-pixel alpha tiles use uncompressed RGBA. The conventional atlas sizes run from 16 to 256 pixels. The leader scene uses a static diplomacy fallback. The extracted DDS art was reviewed as a contact sheet for crop, transparency and legibility.

## ImageGen prompts

### Scene sheet

> Original strategy game key art sheet, 2 by 2 grid, exactly equal frameless rectangular quadrants with crisp divisions centered on both axes, canvas landscape 3:2. Each image is independent and fills its quadrant. No lettering, no numbers, no borders. Top left: gigantic real emperor scorpion as the intelligent Overlord, raised segmented tail and pincers, glossy black exoskeleton traced with gold, low symmetrical view in underground royal chamber under amber light. Top right: Dawn of Man landscape, immense subterranean obsidian fortress with amber crystals and ancient molting chambers, black emperor scorpion Overlord prominent in front and smaller elite scorpions emerging from gates, wide grand composition. Bottom left: top-down fantasy cartography of a desert dominion with black outcrops, ridges, central amber citadel, hand-painted antique parchment, no text or labels. Bottom right: a massive well-lit black and amber emperor scorpion with its pincers and raised stinger in a low three-quarter combat stance, very clear shape at small sizes, isolated on dark bronze smoky stone. A cohesive amber, bronze and obsidian palette, detailed polished painterly Civilization V era strategy game illustrations.

### Emblem sheet

> Production 3x3 icon sprite sheet for original amber obsidian Scorpion Dominion Civilization V mod. Perfect equal square cells, all aligned to exact thirds in a square image, center each bold chunky emblem with generous margins and no symbols crossing cells, solid near black behind every icon. Nine extremely distinct readable high contrast golden silhouettes on black, flat carved metallic heraldry with amber illumination, no text, numbers or border grid. In reading order, row 1: a recognizable scorpion with full legs, symmetrical raised pincers and a high curved hooked tail, this first icon is a clean filled golden silhouette against pure black (no rim); wide stepped underground black stone molting chamber with luminous amber chrysalis arch; a golden stylized scorpion shedding a second broken husk. Row 2: huge aggressive rust-orange open crushing pincers pointing upwards; blue-grey obsidian shield with golden trim blocking three arrows; a brilliant gold serrated crown enclosing a raised curved stinger. Row 3: chitin breastplate with broad layered dark plates and bright amber contours; militant scorpion sigil flanked by angular pincer wings; stone funerary tablet under a golden laurel wreath and fallen stinger emblem. Each symbol fits entirely within a circular area that is 80 percent of the size of its square. All designed to be legible even at 32 pixels.
